# Skip lines the nightly integration suite may print. scripts/ci/check-no-skips.py
# reads every test's captured output from the nextest JUnit report and fails the
# shard on any skip line (one starting with skip, skipping, skipped or SKIP)
# that no entry here allows. An entry is
#
#   TEST_GLOB  LINE_GLOB
#
# matched, ignoring case, with fnmatch globs against `<binary id>::<test name>`
# and the skip line; LINE_GLOB may contain spaces. Give each entry its reason,
# keep it as narrow as the lane, and delete it once the nightly installs what
# the lane needs.
